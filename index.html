The show

SILEX runs a live music review on TikTok, Tuesday through Sunday at 5 PM Mountain. Independent and unsigned artists submit a track, it plays in full to the room, and SILEX scores it on air with notes on the mix, the hook and the performance. Artists rate the host back after every review.

The show runs on Standard, a review platform whose host roster includes Adele's longtime drummer, Sean Paul's keyboardist and diamond-certified industry veterans. SILEX has been the top-ranked host on the platform since it launched.

For brands

An audience of musicians, producers and independent artists who buy audio gear, plugins, distribution and creator tools, and who show up every night, six nights a week. Music-related brands get priority.

Gear seeding, affiliate and creator codes, sponsored segments, sponsored content and long-term ambassadorships are all open.

Current partner: sE Electronics. The DynaCaster is the primary mic on every live.

Ways to work together, on the live show and across socials. Pricing on request.

On the live show

  • Shoutout

    A 60-second on-air mention and your link in bio for one stream.

  • Featured segment

    A 5 to 10 minute on-stream demo or review of the product, a pinned comment through the stream, and one dedicated short-form clip posted after.

  • Sponsored skips

    Your brand covers free skips that SILEX gives to independent artists on air. Every skip is announced in your name, so the artist and the whole room hear who moved them to the front of the line.

  • Partner week

    The featured segment on four streams, a branded overlay on every live that week, and two short-form clips.

Across socials

  • Sponsored reel

    A dedicated short-form video built around your product, posted to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook.

  • Stories and posts

    Instagram and Facebook Stories, posts on X and Threads, and an announcement in the SILEX Discord.
